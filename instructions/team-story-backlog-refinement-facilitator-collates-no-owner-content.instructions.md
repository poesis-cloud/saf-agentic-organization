# Invariant: facilitator_collates_no_owner_content

the facilitator collates the split / testability / clarification challenge findings into the open-item ledger (flow-owned) and dispatches them to @product-owner — the owner reads the committed ledger, never the challengers directly; it rewrites no owner content
