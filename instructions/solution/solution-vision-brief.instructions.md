# Solution Vision (`solution-vision`)

Describe the future state of the large solution: customer and stakeholder needs and the proposed solution.

- **Main artifact:** `solution-vision` at `portfolio/solution-trains/<solution-train-slug>/solution-vision.md` (template `templates/solution/solution-vision.artifact-template.md`), owned by `solution-manager`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/solution-trains/<solution-train-slug>/challenges/solution-vision-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `solution-architect`, `business-owner`, `ux-designer`, `product-marketing-manager`.

## Draft

- Read the Portfolio Vision and the Epics that fund the solution.
- Write for alignment across ARTs and suppliers: the vision statement, customers and needs, solution context.
- Name key capabilities by intent; they are written in `capability-refinement`.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Would two ARTs reading this build compatible things?
- Is the solution context concrete enough to constrain design?

## Revise

Done when: Vision statement, customers and solution context are filled; exclusions are explicit. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

Solution Management's human lead decides whether this is the vision to align the train on. Next: `solution-gate-decision`; then `solution-roadmap` and `capability-refinement`.
