# Solution Demo (`solution-demo`)

Give stakeholders an integrated view of what the ARTs and suppliers built in the PI, as objective evidence, and collect their feedback.

- **Record:** `event-record` at `portfolio/solution-trains/<solution-train-slug>/events/<event-slug>.solution-demo.md` (template `templates/events/solution-demo.artifact-template.md`), with `event: solution-demo`.

## Agenda

- What was integrated across the whole Solution Train, and what was not.
- Each capability demonstrated against its acceptance criteria.
- NFR and compliance evidence; overall fitness for purpose.
- Stakeholder feedback, recorded in their words.

## After the run

`capability-transition` for capabilities accepted; feedback feeds `capability-refinement` and the portfolio's investment decisions.
