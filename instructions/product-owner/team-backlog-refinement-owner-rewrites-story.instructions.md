# Team Backlog Refinement — owner rewrite

The Product Owner rewrites the Story after the Enabler Story and the feasibility challenge. Produce or update the Story artifact only. Absorb the review findings; do not author the review packet. Keep acceptance criteria, size, risk, and `## NFR applicability / trace` explicit. Inherited NFRs stay inherited; do not author the product NFR register.

Surgical Demand: rewrite only if the Story itself was in-scope and challenged. If this run's in-scope main was only the Enabler Story, emit explicit no-change on the business Story rather than a second rewrite.
