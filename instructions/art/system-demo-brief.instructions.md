# System Demo (`system-demo`)

Show the integrated work of all teams for the iteration from a production-like environment, as an objective measure of progress, and collect feedback.

- **Record:** `event-record` at `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/events/<event-slug>.system-demo.md` (template `templates/events/system-demo.artifact-template.md`), with `event: system-demo`.

## Agenda

- What was integrated and demonstrated, per Feature and acceptance criterion.
- What was not ready and why.
- NFR verification results.
- Feedback from Business Owners and stakeholders, recorded in their words.

## After the run

`feature-transition` and `feature-enabler-transition` for the items accepted; feedback feeds `feature-refinement` and `feature-enabler-refinement`.
