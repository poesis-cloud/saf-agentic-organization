# Product Vision (`product-vision`)

Describe the future state of the solution the ART develops: customers, their needs and the features proposed to meet them.

- **Main artifact:** `product-vision` at `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/product-vision.md` (template `templates/art/product-vision.artifact-template.md`), owned by `product-manager`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/challenges/product-vision-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `system-architect`, `ux-designer`, `business-owner`, `product-marketing-manager`.

## Draft

- Read the Portfolio or Solution Vision and the Epics or capabilities that fund the ART.
- Ground customers and problems in evidence; write the vision statement last.
- List key features as candidates; they are written in `feature-refinement`.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Is each need backed by evidence from customers or usage?
- Would the vision help a team choose between two designs?

## Revise

Done when: Vision statement, customers, problems and measures of success are filled. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

Product Management's human lead and the Business Owners decide whether this is the vision. Next: `art-gate-decision`; then `product-roadmap` and `feature-refinement`.
