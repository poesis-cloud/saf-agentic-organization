# PI Roadmap (`product-roadmap`)

Forecast the ART's deliverables: the committed PI and the next two as a forecast.

- **Main artifact:** `product-roadmap` at `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/product-roadmap.md` (template `templates/art/product-roadmap.artifact-template.md`), owned by `product-manager`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/challenges/product-roadmap-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `system-architect`, `business-owner`, `release-manager`.

## Draft

- Read the Product Vision, the Features and their status, and the current PI Objectives.
- The current PI shows what was committed at PI Planning; the next two are a forecast from WSJF and capacity.
- State the capacity allocation between features, enablers and maintenance.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Does the forecast leave capacity for enablers the runway needs?
- Is any forecast presented as a commitment?

## Revise

Done when: Committed and forecast PIs are distinct; capacity allocation is stated. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

Product Management's human lead decides whether to communicate this roadmap. Next: `art-gate-decision`.
