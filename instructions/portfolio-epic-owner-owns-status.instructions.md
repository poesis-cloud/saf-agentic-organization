# Invariant: epic_owner_owns_status

The Epic — including its `status:` — is authored by its owner (@business-owner for business, @enterprise-architect for enabler). @value-management-officer governs the status transition and records it in the @value-management-officer-owned portfolio kanban; it does not write the Epic itself.
