# Iteration Retrospective (`iteration-retrospective`)

Review the team's practices at the end of the iteration and choose the improvements to try next.

- **Record:** `event-record` at `portfolio/arts/<art-slug>/teams/<team-slug>/events/<event-slug>.iteration-retrospective.md` (template `templates/events/iteration-retrospective.artifact-template.md`), with `event: iteration-retrospective`.

## Agenda

- Quantitative review: goals met, velocity, flow.
- Qualitative review: what went well, what did not, from the `friction` notes of the iteration's artifacts.
- One or two improvements to try, each an improvement Story to write.

## After the run

`story-refinement` for each improvement Story.
