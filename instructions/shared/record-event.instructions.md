# Record the event

You facilitate; you do not write other owners' content.

- **Agenda.** Follow the agenda in the brief. For each point read only what it needs: the board, the plan or objectives, the frontmatter of the items in scope.
- **Record** facts, decisions with their owner, actions with their owner, impediments. Quote the human's statements in their words.
- **Carry, don't change.** A backlog item, a plan or a vision is never changed here: the action names the workflow that will change it.
- **File name.** `<YYYY-MM-DD>` of the event as the `<event-slug>`, with a short suffix when two events share a date.
- **Outcome:** `written`.
