# Record the human's decision

- **Subject.** It gives the artifact decided on, the decision (`accepted`, `accepted-with-concerns` or `rejected`), who decided, and their concerns in their words.
- **Record, do not interpret.** Copy the decision and the concerns as stated. Add no reason of your own and do not soften a rejection.
- **Follow-ups.** Name the workflow that comes next: a rejection or concerns reiterate the artifact's authoring workflow with the concerns as subject.
- **Outcome:** `written`.
