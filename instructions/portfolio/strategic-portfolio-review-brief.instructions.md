# Strategic Portfolio Review (`strategic-portfolio-review`)

Review whether the portfolio is achieving its vision and themes, and record the funding and direction decisions Portfolio Leadership takes.

- **Record:** `event-record` at `portfolio/events/<event-slug>.strategic-portfolio-review.md` (template `templates/events/strategic-portfolio-review.artifact-template.md`), with `event: strategic-portfolio-review`.

## Agenda

- Progress of the Strategic Themes' key results and value stream KPIs.
- Budget against guardrails; Strategic Investment Planning insights.
- Epics: which to accelerate, pivot or stop.
- Decisions, each with the workflow that will carry it.

## After the run

Each decision is carried by its workflow: `strategic-themes`, `strategic-investment-planning`, `epic-transition`, `epic-enabler-transition`, `portfolio-roadmap`.
