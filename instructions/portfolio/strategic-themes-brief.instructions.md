# Strategic Themes (`strategic-themes`)

Formulate or revise the portfolio's Strategic Themes as Objectives and Key Results that connect the portfolio to the enterprise strategy.

- **Main artifact:** `strategic-themes` at `portfolio/strategic-themes.md` (template `templates/portfolio/strategic-themes.artifact-template.md`), owned by `portfolio-manager`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/challenges/strategic-themes-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `enterprise-architect`, `business-owner`, `financial-controller`, `data-analyst`.

## Draft

- Read the human's strategy input and, when they exist, the Portfolio Vision and Lean Budgets.
- State each theme as one objective with two to five measurable key results. A theme differentiates: business as usual is not a theme.
- Say what each theme changes in budgets, the vision and the backlog, and what the portfolio will not pursue.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Does each key result measure an outcome, with a baseline and a target, rather than an activity?
- Do the themes make choices, or could any portfolio claim them?

## Revise

Done when: Every theme has measurable key results; exclusions are explicit. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

Portfolio Leadership decides whether these themes are the portfolio's direction. Next: `portfolio-gate-decision` to record the decision; then `portfolio-vision` and `strategic-investment-planning`.
