# Portfolio Roadmap (`portfolio-roadmap`)

Build or revise the multi-year view of how the Portfolio Vision will be achieved across the value streams.

- **Main artifact:** `portfolio-roadmap` at `portfolio/portfolio-roadmap.md` (template `templates/portfolio/portfolio-roadmap.artifact-template.md`), owned by `portfolio-manager`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/challenges/portfolio-roadmap-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `enterprise-architect`, `financial-controller`, `product-marketing-manager`.

## Draft

- Read the Portfolio Vision, the Epics and their status, and the solution and PI roadmaps that exist.
- Near term is committed (Epics implementing or ready); mid term is a forecast; long term is intent without dates that read as commitments.
- Aggregate what the trains forecast; do not invent their dates.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Does the near term fit the funded capacity of the value streams?
- Which milestone is a real external date, and which is only a wish?

## Revise

Done when: The three horizons are distinct; every near-term item traces to an approved Epic. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

Portfolio Leadership decides whether to communicate this roadmap. Next: `portfolio-gate-decision`.
