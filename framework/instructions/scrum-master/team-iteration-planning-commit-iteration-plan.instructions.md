# Iteration Planning — commit iteration plan

The Scrum Master rewrites and commits the Iteration Plan / Iteration Goals after the feasibility challenge.

Inputs: the drafted plan, the feasibility-review packet, refined Stories, PI Objectives, and the Team Manifest.
Output: the plan artifact (Iteration Goals and the selected Story list). Absorb the review findings; do not author the review packet. Do not author or rewrite Stories.
