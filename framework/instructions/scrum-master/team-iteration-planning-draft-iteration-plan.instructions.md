# Iteration Planning — draft iteration plan

The Scrum Master drafts the Iteration Plan / Iteration Goals from already refined Stories.

Inputs: Stories and Enabler Stories from Team Backlog Refinement, PI Objectives, and the Team Manifest.
Output: the plan artifact as a proposal (Iteration Goals and the selected Story list). Do not author or rewrite Stories. Do not treat the draft as the iteration commit until the feasibility challenge has landed and the owner rewrite runs.
