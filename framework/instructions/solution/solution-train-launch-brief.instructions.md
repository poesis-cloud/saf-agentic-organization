# Solution Train launch (`solution-train-launch`)

Define or revise one Solution Train: its mission, ARTs and suppliers, roles and cadence.

- **Main artifact:** `solution-train-canvas` at `portfolio/solution-trains/<solution-train-slug>/solution-train-canvas.md` (template `templates/solution/solution-train-canvas.artifact-template.md`), owned by `solution-train-engineer`.
- **Challenge packets:** `portfolio/solution-trains/<solution-train-slug>/challenges/solution-train-launch-<your-agent-slug>.challenge.md` (template `templates/shared/challenge.artifact-template.md`).
- **Panel:** `agile-coach`, `solution-architect`, `business-analyst`.

## Draft

- Read the value stream definition and the ART canvases that exist.
- Start with the foundational elements: vision owner, backlog owner, roles; the Kanban, roadmap and System Team can follow.
- Put every ART and supplier on one PI cadence.

## Challenge

Apply your own lens (your skill has a section for this artifact), and also test:

- Do the ARTs and suppliers named cover the whole solution, with clear interfaces?
- Is anyone accountable for integration across trains?

## Revise

Done when: Mission, members, roles and the common cadence are filled. Every packet of this run has its `challengeLedger` entry.

## Human gate

The solution's leaders decide whether to operate the train this way. Next: `solution-gate-decision`; then `solution-vision` and `solution-intent`.
